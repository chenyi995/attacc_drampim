\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

\Fugue{} is evaluated in five parts, the balanced frequency
point that sets the PE clock, the area of the additions,
the end-to-end model performance on
the constructed configurations, an ablation across the
seven-rung ladder on those configurations, and a confirmation on
real serving workloads.

\subsection{The Balanced Frequency Point}
\label{sec:eval-balance}

The bank PE (\texttt{mq\_bank\_pe}, a $16$-lane MAC with its
operand store and control) is synthesized across a frequency
sweep in ASAP7~\cite{asap7}, and its per-operation energy is
the constant of the simulator's energy model.
In the interval of Equation~(\ref{eq:interval}) the compute
term meets the energy term at the balanced frequency point
$f_{\mathrm{PE}}^\star=1.30$\,GHz.
At that point, one column read serves the resident queries in
$8$ cycles (Figure~\ref{fig:balance}), gives $2\times$ the
throughput of the stock PE running the same queries, and
keeps the SRAM unchanged.
Past $f_{\mathrm{PE}}^\star$ the budget pins the interval, so
a faster clock adds only area.


\subsection{Area}
\label{sec:eval-area}

\begin{figure}[t]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{fig/ver1/figoverhead.pdf}
  \figexpl{One DRAM die by area. The storage of its $128$ banks
  takes almost all of it, and what the two designs put beside
  the arrays is the rest; \Fugue{}'s two segments are
  increments over AttAcc's.}
  \caption{The area composition of one DRAM die.}
  \label{fig:overhead}
  \Description{One stacked bar of a DRAM die, almost all of it
  bank storage, followed by thin segments for AttAcc's bank and
  bank-group logic, the increments Fugue adds to each, and the
  unused remainder.}
\end{figure}

Synthesis rolls the units \Fugue{} adds up to one stack
(Section~\ref{sec:method-syn}, Figure~\ref{fig:overhead}).
A DRAM die is $121$\,mm$^2$~\cite{hbm3jssc} and the storage of
its $128$ banks takes $106.2$ of
them~\cite{papi,hbm3jssc}, so every unit either design places
beside the arrays lives in the $14.8$\,mm$^2$ that are left.
AttAcc spends $8.28$ of that and \Fugue{} $9.93$, so serving
the shared form costs $1.64$\,mm$^2$ per die and leaves the
die $96.0\%$ full.

The additions land unevenly.
The bank PE grows by $18.34\%$, from $6{,}205$ to
$7{,}343$\,\textmu m$^2$, which is what running the same
RTL at the balanced frequency point costs, over the same
operand store and the same SRAM
(Section~\ref{sec:eval-balance}).
The bank-group accumulator and its buffer grow by $54.36\%$,
holding one partial score and one partial context per
resident query.

The bank and the bank group carry the multiply and the add,
and both are FP16.
The softmax is the one unit in FP32, which is why it
dominates the buffer die.

The two levels on the buffer die are small enough to state
outright.
\Fugue{}'s logic die is $1.620$\,mm$^2$, of which $1.578$ is
the softmax array whose buffer holds one score vector per
active agent, $0.025$ the diff decoder with the
$\mathcal{D}_i$ bitmap, its write filter and mask gate,
$0.016$ the sixteen channel accumulators, and $0.0005$ the
causal comparator.
The HBM controller adds a $0.0058$\,mm$^2$ table that
translates a compacted diff token's logical position.
Both sit on the buffer die in a logic process, so neither
carries the DRAM density factor, and together they are
$1.4\%$ of the die area \Fugue{} occupies.
The softmax array is synthesized whole, all $16$ channels with
their buffers, and meets $1.3$\,GHz.

\subsection{End-to-End Model Performance}
\label{sec:eval-e2e}

The end-to-end experiment runs the fourteen workload
configurations of Section~\ref{sec:method-workloads} on the
six models of Section~\ref{sec:method-models}.
It reports the time to first token per prefill, the per-token
decode latency, and the served throughput of \Fugue{} (A6)
against the AttAcc reference (A1) and the GPU-software point
(A2), with the KV bytes each holds against the no-reuse
layout.

\subsection{Ablation across the Placement Ladder}
\label{sec:eval-ladder}

On the same configurations the full ladder isolates one
change per rung.
A2 over A1 is software sharing alone, and A3b over A2 is
decode in the banks with the shared KV resident there,
appended and rotated as a naive store does.
A4c over A3b is the packed diff rows rotating over the head's
own channels, A4e over A4c is the placement table, for the
master rows in place of the write-order rotation and for the
diff rows grouped per agent, A5 over A4e is
prefill attention in the banks with the shared scans it
enables, and A6 over A5 is the dynamic per-prefill placement
rule.
The same metrics are reported at each rung, together with the
fraction of prefills the rule sends to the banks, so each
source of the end-to-end result is attributed to one change.

\subsection{Real Workloads}
\label{sec:eval-real}

The result is confirmed on three production traces, a
Mooncake~\cite{mooncake} tool-and-agent trace,
ShareGPT~\cite{sharegpt} multi-turn conversations, and
MultiHop-RAG~\cite{multihoprag} queries over shared documents
(Section~\ref{sec:method-traces}), running the three endpoints
of the ladder, A1, A2, and A6.
At A6 the upstream policy is swept over
CacheBlend~\cite{cacheblend}, Cache-Craft~\cite{cachecraft},
and CacheTune~\cite{cachetune}, each with its own diff
density, so the placement result is read as a function of the
software layer rather than at one policy.
