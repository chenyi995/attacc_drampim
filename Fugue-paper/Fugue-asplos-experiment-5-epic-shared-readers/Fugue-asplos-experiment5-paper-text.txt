We construct a synchronous fan-out of one, two and four readers over the same EPIC 8K documents, retaining kvlink-16 and using actual native batched GPU operators and PIM head traces. With four readers, F4 reduces TTFT from 138.735 to 88.635 ms and E2E latency from 235.799 to 185.700 ms relative to F3, reductions of 36.11% and 21.25%. Modeled E2E dynamic energy decreases by 6.27%; decode time and energy are identical for F2--F4. Shared/diff storage reduces peak remote KV payload by 77.94% relative to F2 full materialization. The source pool is counted once, while private versions and all reader scans are charged. Native per-request address views are retained, so this experiment does not claim physical shared-address hotspot measurements or cross-reader scan coalescing.
