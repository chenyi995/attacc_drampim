Experiment 2 evaluates MQ/GPU sensitivity on a Q-by-bandwidth grid and a Q-by-cache grid, holding the remaining parameter fixed. The common signed reduction scale shows both the winner and the magnitude of its advantage. The figures use evaluated cells without smoothing; detailed integer crossings remain in Experiment 1. Cache readback adds a C-dependent GPU cost, while PIM incurs Q input, output return, and exposed new-KV transfer. Consequently, link bandwidth affects both alternatives, and increasing bandwidth need not monotonically favor the same device. These are conditional service-cost results under PIM-resident cache, not a universal token-count rule.
