Prefill attention service latency with resident KV reuse. The left and middle columns compare GPU execution with ordinary PIM (top) and MQ PIM (bottom) at zero and 1,024 cached tokens. The right column fixes 1,024 cached tokens and uses the repository's maximum and minimum interface presets, at 450 and 32 GB/s one way. The other panels use 300 GB/s. Numbers label integer device-switch thresholds confirmed at adjacent Q values; a range marks repeated crossings resolved by an integer-Q sweep, not statistical uncertainty. Cached KV initially resides on PIM. Service costs include Q/output transfers and the portion of new-KV transfer not hidden by scanning. All panels retain A100a compute parameters; MQ supports eight resident queries at approximately 1.3 GHz with an eight-tCK full-group command interval. Panels with crossings use focused Q windows to display both regimes; single-winner cold-prefill panels retain the full sweep. Background tints indicate the faster device. Full-range plots and all samples are retained in the supplement.
