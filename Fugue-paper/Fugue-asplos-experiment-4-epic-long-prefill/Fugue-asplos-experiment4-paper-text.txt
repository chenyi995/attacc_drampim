We replay EPIC LongContext inputs at 4K, 8K and 16K source tokens with its kvlink-16 query-selection rule on the native AttAcc LLAMA-7B/A100a model. F4 reduces TTFT relative to GPU-prefill F3 by 37.85%, 31.67% and 20.43%, respectively, and E2E latency by 7.57%, 10.72% and 11.24% at a fixed sixteen-token output cap. At 16K, attention kernels account for 57.96% of F3 TTFT, rising to 81.95% when the exposed cached-KV readback is included. MQ PIM kernel time alone is slightly higher than GPU kernel time at this point; its lower complete service cost comes from avoiding remote cached-KV readback. These are native cost-model replays with adapted tokenization, not numerical EPIC inference or answer-quality measurements.
