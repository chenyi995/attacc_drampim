## Appendix: definitions & rung legend

- **arrival(req)** = start of its tier (`summary.tiers[tier].start_s`, cross-checked against batch `q_arrival_s`); prefill and e2e are measured from it, so they exclude the wait for upstream tiers. prefill = `prefill_end_s - arrival`, decode = `end_s - first_token_s`, e2e = `end_s - arrival`.

- Energy `by_event` names carry the phase: events starting `decode` are the decode pass, all others (qkv, gpu_ff*, pim_kv_scan_*, gpu_prefill_*, KV placement) are the one-shot prefill pass, so prefill + decode = total. `by_class` splits the same total by unit (GPU/PIM/LINK/DIE/TLB). Neither carries a tier tag, so the energy table is per rung only.

- The **KV over link** column in Extras is the headline of the in-memory story: A2 (GPU-only) streams the whole KV every turn, the PIM rungs keep it in the banks.

- Rungs: **A1** AttAcc dense no-reuse (decode in banks, prefill GPU); **A2** GPU-only software reuse; **A3** decode in banks over the append-order layout; **A3a** + write-mask; **A4** + split-channel master-diff layout; **A5** + all prefill in banks; **A6** + dynamic per-prefill placement rule.
