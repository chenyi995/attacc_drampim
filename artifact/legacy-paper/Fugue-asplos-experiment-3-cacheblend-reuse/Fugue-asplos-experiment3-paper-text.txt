We replay two complete CacheBlend demo inputs selected solely by minimum tokenized prompt length, retaining its full first layer, full-QKV check layer, and 16% partial recomputation thereafter. All variants use the same original AttAcc layout and operator model. F3 reduces the peak remote KV footprint by 26.18% relative to full materialization. F4 selects GPU for the first two layers and MQ PIM for the remaining thirty, reducing mean TTFT by 3.35% and E2E latency by 2.00% relative to F3. Native PIM decode is identical for F2--F4, so their TBT and decode energy do not improve with sharing. We separately report initialization cost and the sensitivity to overlapping F2's full KV export; this isolates the capacity result from the native baseline's export schedule.
